Predictive Scoring · Budget Attribution

Lead Intelligence

Problem

  • Low confidence in lead prioritization: high false-negative rate on qualified prospects
  • No propensity-based link between lead quality and campaign budget allocation
  • Marketo-to-Dynamics CRM lead drop-off was unmeasured

Objective

  • Classify leads accurately and apply lead scoring across the prospect CRM database
  • Optimize campaign-level budget allocation using total leads, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead, etc.
  • Map first-touch to last-touch user journey: determine true campaign efficiency

Action

  • Engineered predictive lead qualification models (Python, XGBoost, scikit-learn): cut false-negative misclassifications by 20-22%
  • Built propensity-based campaign budget scoring models, projected 28-32% ROI uplift at flat spend
  • Applied budget allocation model and grew total web leads by 20% QoQ and qualified leads by 15% QoQ

Tools & Methods

Python / XGBoost / scikit-learn / Microsoft Dynamics CRM

Outcome

20-22%reduction in false-negative lead misclassifications
28-32%projected ROI uplift at flat spend
+20%QoQ growth in total web leads
+15%QoQ growth in qualified leads

Engagement Modeling · Segmentation

Engagement Prediction - Segmentation

Problem

  • Campaign gifts, mailers, emails drove prospect appointments, but no visibility into who was engaging
  • Unclear if engagement was random or concentrated by characteristics
  • No way to identify contacts worth deeper investment, so campaigns kept reaching low-likelihood audiences

Objective

  • Identify what separates engaged from non-engaged contacts, across prospect and customer audiences
  • Segment by engagement likelihood to guide targeting, escalation, or deprioritization

Action

  • Engineered firmographic and behavioral features across prospect and customer datasets
  • Logistic regression and XGBoost to flag engagement drivers and handle class imbalance
  • K-prototypes clustering surfaced 3 segments and applied to campaign targeting and messaging

Tools & Methods

Python / statsmodels / XGBoost / K-Prototypes

Outcome

+10 ptsmailer engagement rate for prospects
+17 ptsappointment-set rate for prospects
+0.8 ptsCTR across matched campaign types

Ad Performance Lift · IPW

Causal Inference - A/B Testing

Problem

  • Digital marketing team needed to know whether a new ad campaign meaningfully increased conversion
  • Naive comparison overstates lift: treated users skew more active and higher-converting to begin with

Objective

  • Quantify causal impact of ad exposure on conversion using various statistical methods
  • Recommend strategy based on data-driven lift estimates, not naive comparisons

Action

  • 2M users randomization-checked to validate experimental design
  • Average Treatment Effect on the Treated (ATT: 26.2%) and Intent-to-Treat (ITT: 23.8%) projected
  • Inverse Probability Weighting (IPW), Double Machine Learning, and regression applied to control for covariates such as prior conversion behavior, activity level, income

Tools & Methods

Python / statsmodels / IPW / Double ML

Outcome

+78.7%relative lift in conversion from ad exposure (11.8% absolute)
+25%projected ROI from retargeting high-activity, previously-converted users
