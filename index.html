Lead Intelligence In Progress

Problem

The sales team had low confidence in lead prioritization — high false-negative rates meant genuinely qualified prospects were being deprioritized or missed.

Objective

Improve lead-scoring accuracy and campaign budget allocation across the funnel, without increasing total spend.

Action

Engineered predictive lead qualification models (Python, XGBoost, scikit-learn) across a multi-million-record CRM database. Built propensity-based campaign budget scoring models, and ran segmentation and clustering across CRM and campaign data to surface funnel bottlenecks.

Outcome (in progress)

A/B Testing · Causal Inference

A/B Testing - Ad Performance Lift

Problem

A digital marketing team needed to know whether exposing users to a new online ad campaign meaningfully increased conversion, and how that effect varied across user segments.

Objective

Quantify the causal impact of ad exposure on conversion using rigorous statistical methods, and recommend strategy based on reliable lift estimates rather than naive comparisons.

Action

Ran randomization checks to validate the experimental design across 2M users, then calculated Average Treatment Effect on the Treated (ATT: 26.2%) and Intent-to-Treat (ITT: 23.8%). Applied Inverse Probability Weighting, Double Machine Learning, and regression models to control for covariates like prior conversion behavior, activity level, and income.

Outcome

+11.8%absolute conversion lift from ad exposure (78.7% relative)
+25%projected ROI from retargeting high-activity, previously-converted users
