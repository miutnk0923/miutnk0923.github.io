Ad Spend Optimization

Google Ads Performance Optimization — SEO

Problem

A ringtone company was wasting nearly half its ad budget. Of 40 targeted keywords, 16 generated zero or negative ROI — 47% of total spend.

Objective

Improve ad spend efficiency by identifying high-performing keywords, reallocating budget strategically, and lifting ROI by at least 5%.

Action

Identified that keyword position had a strong negative correlation with conversion. Filtered out 16 underperforming keywords and reallocated spend based on individual ROI contribution and optimal bid position.

Outcome

+8% lift in ROI, surpassing the 5% goal
0% of budget left on underperforming keywords after reallocation
