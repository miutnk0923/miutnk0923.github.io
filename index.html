Predictive Analytics · Marketing Attribution

Lead Intelligence: Predictive Scoring & Budget Attribution — In Progress

Problem

Budget and targeting decisions relied on descriptive, after-the-fact reporting — campaign performance was only understood once spend was already committed, and lead quality wasn't assessed until leads had already entered the CRM.

Objective

Build predictive analytics and attribution intelligence that shifts decision-making from hindsight to foresight — forecasting lead quality before spend is committed, enabling smarter budget allocation across paid and organic channels without increasing total spend.

Action

Cleaned and structured a large multi-source lead dataset, diagnosed the patterns behind disqualified leads, and built a first-touch/last-touch attribution framework to separate campaigns that generate initial interest ("originators") from campaigns that convert leads into qualified appointments ("closers") — clarifying which channels deserve more investment and why.

Outcome (in progress)

~+7% quarter-over-quarter growth in lead volume after applying early model findings to budget reallocation
~+21% quarter-over-quarter growth in qualified leads — nearly 3x the raw lead-volume lift
~+13% relative improvement in qualified rate, indicating the reallocation improved lead quality, not just volume. Full predictive model (Phase 5) still in development.

Causal Inference · A/B Testing

Quantifying Ad Exposure Lift with Inverse Probability Weighting (IPW)

Problem

A digital marketing team needed to know whether exposing users to a new ad campaign actually increased conversions — and how that effect varied across user segments — rather than relying on a naive before/after comparison.

Objective

Quantify the true causal impact of ad exposure on conversion using rigorous statistical methods, producing reliable lift estimates the team could confidently act on.

Action

Designed and analyzed a randomized controlled experiment across ~2M users. After validating randomization, calculated Average Treatment Effect on the Treated (ATT) and Intent-to-Treat (ITT), then applied Inverse Probability Weighting (IPW) alongside regression and Double Machine Learning models to control for covariates like prior conversion behavior, activity level, and income — isolating the true causal effect of ad exposure from simple correlation.

Outcome

26.2% Average Treatment Effect on the Treated (ATT)
78.7% relative lift in conversions from ad exposure, validating ad effectiveness beyond naive measurement
+25% projected ROI improvement by targeting high-activity, previously-converted users specifically
Category — e.g. Experimentation / A-B Testing
